# AI News Roundup — November 12, 2026

### Top stories from the past 24 hours

1. **Nvidia board prepares to oust CEO Jensen Huang amid dual-affair scandal and spy allegations**  
   Nvidia’s board is reportedly preparing to remove longtime CEO Jensen Huang after revelations of two extramarital affairs—one with Alice Huang, a Megaspeed executive suspected by U.S. intelligence of ties to Chinese intelligence services, and another with Nvidia healthcare VP Kimberly Powell. The board hired Wachtell Lipton for an investigation, is meeting to decide his fate, and is working with an outside firm on succession. Shares fell ~8% after hours (hundreds of billions in market value wiped out recently). Huang has no clear successor and has long resisted formal succession planning.  
   Sources: [Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/business/nvidia-is-preparing-to-oust-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-30f421ad), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/commentary/breakingviews/jensen-huangs-fall-can-shatter-nvidias-kingdom-2026-11-12/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/11/nvidias-stock-drops-as-board-reportedly-preps-to-oust-ceo-jensen-huang/), [The Guardian live](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/live/2026/nov/12/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-affairs-share-price-plunges-live-latest)

2. **Anthropic advances blockbuster IPO at $70–$78 range, up to $1.4 trillion valuation**  
   Anthropic continues its roadshow with a confirmed price range of $70 to $78 per share for 825 million Class A shares. At the top end this implies a fully diluted valuation of ~$1.405 trillion and raises up to ~$64.35 billion, plus a concurrent $26 billion private placement with strategic investors including Google, Nvidia, and TSMC. Trading is expected around November 19 on Nasdaq as ANT. Run-rate revenue has hit roughly $81 billion.  
   Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-ipo-price-range-of-70-to-78-per-share-to-reach-an-implied-valuation-of-1-404-trillion/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-price-range-of-70-to-78-in-upcoming-ipo.html), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/anthropic-ipo-raise-much-644-billion-latest-draft-prospectus-shows-2026-11-10/)

3. **UK AI chip startup Fractile raises $720 million at $7 billion valuation**  
   London-based Fractile, developing memory-first inference chips that stack memory closer to logic for up to 10x faster LLM inference and lower energy use, closed a $720 million Series C led by Thrive Capital, Founders Fund, Lightspeed, and Redpoint. Backers include Britain’s Sovereign AI Fund. The valuation jumped from ~$1.1 billion in May; first chips are targeted for next year, with Anthropic reportedly interested as a customer.  
   Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/11/with-720m-round-britains-fractile-gets-a-leg-up-in-the-race-for-ai-chips/), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/uk-backed-ai-chip-startup-fractile-hits-7-billion-valuation-funding-round-2026-11-11/), [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/11/inference-chip-developer-fractile-raises-720m-in-funding/)

4. **Google DeepMind releases AlphaGenome 2 for cell-level genetic variant prediction**  
   AlphaGenome 2 predicts how DNA variations affect gene expression across individual cell types, identifying nearly twice as many influential variants as its predecessor. Trained on over 100 million cell profiles, it is available for non-commercial research via the AlphaGenome API but not yet for clinical diagnosis.  
   Sources: [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphagenome-2-deciphering-how-cells-read-dna-to-better-understand-disease/), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1031623/united-unauthorized-ai-agents) (via Neuron digest)

5. **Anthropic rolls out Claude Sheets and Whiteboards in beta; Claude Data generally available**  
   Building on recent workplace tools, Anthropic launched Claude Sheets (collaborative spreadsheets) and Claude Whiteboards (shared planning boards) in beta for paid plans. Claude Data (formerly Dashboards) is now generally available. Claude Meetings is also expanding.  
   Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-launches-claude-meetings-to-turn-workplace-discussions-into-plans-and-tasks/), [Crypto Briefing / PANews summaries](https://www.sharpe.ai/news/ai)

6. **United Airlines blocks unauthorized AI agents from booking flights**  
   United will allow AI assistants to show options but requires human completion of purchases, citing privacy, accidental bookings, aviation rules, and desire to avoid middlemen. This sets an early boundary as agentic AI expands into real-world transactions.  
   Sources: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1031623/united-unauthorized-ai-agents), [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-thursday-november-12-2026/)

7. **Microsoft AI system uncovers 21-year-old Linux kernel bug among 59 issues**  
   Microsoft’s AI-driven MDASH scanning system identified 59 security issues in the Linux kernel, including a long-hidden cls_u32 shift-out-of-bounds flaw (CVE-2026-74544) unnoticed for at least 21 years.  
   Sources: [Resilience Intel / GBHackers](https://news.resiliencesecurities.com/news/ai)
