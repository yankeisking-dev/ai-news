# AI News Roundup – November 8, 2026

A clean summary of the top AI stories from the past 24 hours.

## 1. OpenAI Closes $11 Billion Tranche at $1.25 Trillion Valuation
OpenAI has closed the first $11 billion of a funding round that values the company at $1.25 trillion, according to reports from Bloomberg and Reuters. The company had initially sought up to $30 billion at a higher valuation (around $1.4T–$1.5T) but scaled back amid a broader AI stock sell-off and post-midterm market concerns. The round could still reach as much as $20 billion. Investors include Coatue, T. Rowe Price, SoftBank, Sequoia, Founders Fund, and Andreessen Horowitz.

**Sources:** [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-06/openai-completes-11-billion-portion-of-funding-at-1-25-trillion-value), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-secures-11-billion-funding-125-trillion-valuation-bloomberg-news-reports-2026-11-06/)

## 2. Anthropic Presses Ahead with IPO as Valuation Expectations Fall
Anthropic continues toward a pre-Thanksgiving IPO despite a tech sell-off. Prospective investors are discussing valuations in the $1.1 trillion to $1.5 trillion range, down from earlier talk of $2 trillion. The company has filed its S-1 and aims to list on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. Strong revenue growth (Q3 around $17B) contrasts with market caution over AI infrastructure spend and political headwinds.

**Sources:** [Bloomberg via Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/companies/anthropic-ipo-buyers-see-valuation-as-low-as-1-1-trillion-14073828.html), various reports from The Decoder and others.

## 3. Claude Code “Don’t Ask Again” Feature Leaks Thousands of Secrets on GitHub
Security firm Apiiro reported that Anthropic’s Claude Code coding agent stored full shell commands (including passwords, API keys, and tokens) in `.claude/settings.local.json` when users selected “Yes, and don’t ask again.” Scans of over 100,000 public copies found 1,122 files with live secrets; broader analysis (including git history) uncovered 2,539 distinct secrets across 1,994 repositories. Anthropic has pushed a fix to gitignore the file going forward, but existing exposures require credential rotation.

**Sources:** [Apiiro Blog](https://apiiro.com/blog/coding-agents-leaking-secrets-github-dont-ask-again), [The Hacker News / AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)

## 4. OpenAI Launches GPT-6 Cyber with “Critical” Cybersecurity Rating
OpenAI released GPT-6 Cyber, a specialized model for authorized vulnerability research and exploit development, limited to vetted Daybreak Red customers. Rated “Critical” under OpenAI’s Preparedness Framework, it showed major gains on ExploitGym (48.5% vs. 32.1% for the prior model) and improved in-scope behavior (99.93% compliance without extra prompts). Researchers used it to find 81 new high-severity Firefox bugs and develop exploits for 948 Linux kernel vulnerabilities.

**Sources:** [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-cyber/), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/openai-launches-gpt-6-cyber-with-critical-cybersecurity-rating-2026-11-05/)

## 5. US Midterms Deliver Blow to AI Data Center Expansion
Democrats’ midterm gains, including control of Congress and several governorships, were driven in part by voter backlash against data centers. Candidates who campaigned on restricting or regulating AI infrastructure won key races. Voters opposed new data centers in their communities by large margins; ballot measures largely rejected projects. Industry observers expect tighter scrutiny, potential moratoriums at state/local levels, and political risk to the multi-hundred-billion-dollar build-out.

**Sources:** [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/07/politics/democrats-midterms-data-centers-ken-paxton-james-talarico), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/7/how-the-us-midterm-elections-reflected-resistance-to-data-centres), [Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/11/04/data-centers-are-midterms-big-loser-01145171)

## 6. AI Buyers Strain Chip Supply Chains; Microchip Lead Times Stretch to 32 Weeks
Microchip Technology’s CEO reported that AI-related buyers “took over the whole factory” at assembly and test houses, pushing other customers out and extending lead times to 32 weeks. Shares fell on the news, highlighting how concentrated AI demand is disrupting broader semiconductor supply.

**Sources:** AI Weekly / 247wallst coverage of recent earnings commentary.
