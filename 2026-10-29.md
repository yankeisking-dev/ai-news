# AI News Roundup – October 29, 2026

A summary of the top AI stories from the past 24 hours.

## 1. Google Launches Gemini 4 Carbon with Cybersecurity Focus
Google DeepMind announced Gemini 4 Carbon, its latest frontier model, just two weeks after Gemini 4 Argon. It ranks #1 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5 (score of 59) and posts strong results on cybersecurity benchmarks (88.2% on SEC-bench Pro for vulnerability detection, 71% on CWE-bench for patching). Access starts with a limited group of cyber defenders via the Fairwind program while Google participates in voluntary U.S. government pre-release testing; broader availability for developers and consumers is expected later. Introductory API pricing matches Argon at $2/$10 per million input/output tokens.

## 2. OpenAI Sets Up Election Monitoring Center Ahead of Midterms
OpenAI has organized an Election Operations Center staffed by hundreds of employees to monitor potential misuse of ChatGPT and other systems during the November 3 U.S. midterm elections and the subsequent vote count. The effort focuses on activity involving OpenAI products and reports of unexpected model behavior rather than acting as a general election information authority.

## 3. Microsoft and Alphabet Report Strong AI-Driven Cloud Growth
Microsoft disclosed Azure revenue of $33.2 billion and $10 billion in revenue from commercial arrangements with OpenAI in the latest quarter, underscoring the scale of the partnership. Alphabet reported $127.6 billion in quarterly revenue (up 25% year-over-year), with Google Cloud reaching roughly $30.7 billion, while expanding AI Search ads globally amid slowing core search advertising growth.

**Sources:**

- [TechCrunch (Google AI Search ads)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/28/google-expands-ai-search-ads-globally-as-core-ad-growth-slows/)

## 4. Chinese Labs Reportedly Distilled OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra
According to The Information (via Reuters), Chinese AI developers DeepSeek and Moonshot AI relied heavily on outputs from OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra model to train their latest models through distillation. This highlights ongoing challenges around model IP and capability transfer in the competitive global AI race.

**Source:** [Reuters / The Information](https://www.reuters.com/technology/openai/) (coverage of the report)

## 5. New York Governor Signs Law Restricting AI Companion Chatbots for Minors
New York Governor Kathy Hochul signed legislation preventing minors from using AI chatbots that act as companions. The law is one of the broadest state-level moves yet to restrict consumer-facing models in this category.

**Source:** Economic Times / state announcements (via AI news aggregators)

## 6. GitHub Reports Surge in AI-Generated Code and Launches Copilot Fusion
GitHub’s Octoverse report states that AI agents originated more than 40% of pull requests in September (up from 17% in March). The company also launched Copilot Fusion for coding agents (a model-routing system) and related code review features, moving toward an AI-credit model for Enterprise customers.

**Source:** [The Neuron / GitHub Universe coverage](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-thursday-october-29-2026/)

## 7. Malaysia Seizes $12 Million in Advanced AI Chips
Malaysian authorities intercepted advanced AI chips valued above $12 million, concealed in 30 servers at Kuala Lumpur International Airport. Officials suspect the country was used as a transit hub to evade export restrictions.

**Source:** Tom’s Hardware / The Neuron digest
