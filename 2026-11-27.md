# AI News Roundup – November 27, 2026

A clean summary of the top AI stories from the past 24 hours.

## 1. Nvidia Halts AI Server Shipments to Southeast Asia
Nvidia has instructed manufacturing partners including Dell and Supermicro to pause shipments of AI servers to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Indonesia. The move follows tightened US Commerce Department export controls designed to close loopholes that allowed Chinese companies to access restricted Nvidia chips via data centers in the region. It is unclear whether the halt is temporary while compliance is sorted out. The four countries have been key routes for Chinese AI developers seeking advanced compute.

**Sources:** [Financial Times](https://www.ft.com/content/7838a715-07d4-4605-aa60-21f5af2b2370), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/nvidia-halts-chip-shipments-southeast-asia-ft-reports-2026-11-26/), [WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/tech/nvidia-tells-partners-to-pause-shipments-to-southeast-asia-5b791cb0)

## 2. Chinese AI Labs Delay Flagship Models Amid US Curbs
Z.AI’s roughly 3-trillion-parameter GLM-5.5 has slipped from a planned November 2026 release to Q1 2027 or later. Moonshot AI’s next model, Kimi K4, may be delayed to mid-2027 as its release cycle stretches toward 12 months. Causes include US export restrictions on advanced chips and anti-distillation measures by OpenAI and Anthropic that limit access to training data. Z.AI released a smaller GLM-5.4 in the interim.

**Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/us-chip-curbs-distillation-crackdown-threaten-chinese-ai-model-development-2026-11-26/), [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/)

## 3. OpenAI Fixes GPT-6.2 Billing Error – Limits Hit Faster
OpenAI corrected a billing bug present since the November 17 launch of GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol. The error had incorrectly discounted usage for most users. After the fix, the same workload now reaches limits 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol compared with last week. Other models were unaffected; no retroactive charges apply. As an apology, Go, Plus, Pro, and Business users receive one additional banked usage reset. OpenAI is also testing a combined Chat and Work experience.

**Sources:** Statement by Thibault Sottiaux on X; [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/openai-token-billing-cost-discount-mistake-sol-astra-2026-11), [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/gpt-6-2-astra-users-now-hit-limits-40-faster-after-openai-patched-a-pricing-error/)

## 4. Tencent Open-Sources DFly2 Speculative Decoder
Tencent’s Hunyuan team open-sourced DFly2, a lightweight 0.85B-parameter speculative decoder that reuses the target model’s sparse KV cache. It delivers up to 81% higher processing throughput for DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash at 64K context length and up to 4.2x speedups on certain agent tasks. The release comes as Chinese developers face tighter compute constraints.

**Sources:** [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-november-27-2026/), Tencent announcements

## 5. Canada Expands AI Compute Access Fund to C$1 Billion
Canada added C$700 million to its AI Compute Access Fund, bringing the total to C$1 billion. The program now covers up to 80% of eligible compute costs for sovereign (Canadian-owned) providers and up to 50% for others. It aims to support domestic model training, inference, and fine-tuning while reducing reliance on foreign clouds. New calls include health and life sciences projects.

**Sources:** [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/canada-invests-700-million-more-to-expand-ai-compute-access-fund/), government announcements via [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-november-27-2026/)

## 6. Huawei Teases Next-Generation HBM Memory Chip
Huawei previewed a next-generation high-bandwidth memory (HBM) chip intended to compete with offerings from Samsung and SK Hynix. HBM provides the rapid data access critical for AI accelerators. The move is part of broader Chinese efforts to build domestic semiconductor capabilities amid US restrictions.

**Sources:** [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-26/huawei-teases-next-gen-ai-memory-chip-to-take-on-samsung-hynix), [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-november-27-2026/)

## 7. SAP Reportedly Weighing ~10,000 Job Cuts in AI Restructuring
SAP is considering cutting at least 10,000 jobs (roughly 9–10% of its global workforce) as it reshapes the company around AI. Details remain preliminary and unconfirmed by the company. The report fits a broader pattern of large software firms adjusting headcount as AI tools change development and operations.

**Sources:** Bloomberg reporting via multiple outlets including [DailyAI](https://dailyai.com/) and [Digest AI](https://digestai.news/today)
