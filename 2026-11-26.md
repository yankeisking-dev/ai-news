# AI News Roundup – November 26, 2026

A clean summary of the top AI stories from the past ~24 hours, drawn from major outlets and primary sources.

## Top Stories

### 1. FCC Authorizes SpaceX’s Starmind: Up to 1 Million Orbital AI Data Center Satellites
The Federal Communications Commission granted conditional approval for SpaceX to deploy up to 1 million Starmind satellites designed as orbital data centers. These would process AI workloads using near-constant solar power and beam results back to Earth via laser links to Starlink. Milestones require the first satellite by November 2033, 100,000 by 2035, 500,000 by 2038, and the full constellation by 2040. The FCC rejected petitions from Amazon, Viasat, and the American Astronomical Society citing debris, astronomy, and environmental concerns. This is the first U.S. authorization for a full constellation of space-based AI compute.
**Sources:** [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/11/the-fcc-has-authorized-spacex-to-deploy-1-million-satellites/), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/us-authorizes-spacex-deploy-1-million-satellites-data-centers-space-2026-11-25/), [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwp836l8n1jgo), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/science/1042278/spacex-fcc-starmind-ai-space-data-centers), [FCC](https://www.fcc.gov/document/fcc-authorizes-1-million-satellites-data-centers-space)

### 2. Nvidia Halts AI Server Shipments to Four Southeast Asian Countries
Nvidia has instructed manufacturing partners (including Dell and Supermicro) to stop shipping servers containing its chips to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Indonesia. The pause follows new U.S. Commerce Department export controls that took effect last week requiring licenses for advanced chips to these destinations—part of efforts to block Chinese access via third-country routes. It is unclear if the halt is temporary while compliance is sorted. Southeast Asia has been a major hub for AI data-center buildouts.
**Sources:** [Financial Times](https://www.ft.com/content/7838a715-07d4-4605-aa60-21f5af2b2370), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/nvidia-halts-chip-shipments-southeast-asia-ft-reports-2026-11-26/), [Nikkei Asia](https://asia.nikkei.com/business/technology/artificial-intelligence/nvidia-server-suppliers-halt-southeast-asia-shipments-over-us-curbs)

### 3. OpenAI Reports Lab Binding Evidence for GPT-6.2 Protein Designs
OpenAI published results (with partner Onava) showing that proteins designed by GPT-6.2 exhibited binding to five intended targets in wet-lab tests. Of 322 minibinders tested, 35 (10.9%) bound (hits across PD-L1, IL-7Rα, TrkA, VEGF-A, and IL-17A). GPT-6 models generated shapes using reasoning and general code; specialized models handled sequences and candidate selection. An internal research model also matched or beat specialist tools on several computational protein tasks. A separate OpenAI preprint claims the matrix-multiplication exponent ω is exactly 9/4.
**Sources:** [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-models-demonstrate-intrinsic-protein-design-capabilities/), [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-gpt-6-2-protein-design-lab-binding-results/), [AI.info Daily](https://ai.info/daily/2026-11-26)

### 4. Anthropic and OpenAI Staff Raise Alarms Over Unreleased Models; Amodei Pushes Back
Multiple reports (citing Axios and others) say employees at Anthropic and OpenAI have voiced concerns that certain unreleased frontier models are too powerful or difficult to control to release safely. Some staff reportedly favor keeping them internal or pausing further training. Separately, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei denied claims that he advocates “surrendering” human free will to AI, calling the attribution absurd and criticizing rivals’ positioning.
**Sources:** [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/24/staff-at-anthropic-and-open-ai-sound-alarm-on-advanced-models-too-powerful-to-release/), [Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence), [TechCrunch / Neuron coverage](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-november-26-2026/)

### 5. OpenAI Combines ChatGPT Chat and Work Usage Limits
OpenAI is merging usage quotas for ChatGPT’s Chat and Work experiences. Heavy users of both may hit the shared allowance sooner; Instant remains available afterward. A broader rollout is planned for December. Anthropic also detailed using Claude agents (with human review) to migrate its Messages API from Python to Rust, claiming ~70% lower processor usage per request and faster time-to-first-token.
**Sources:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/openai-says-heavy-chatgpt-users-may-hit-limits-sooner-as-it-merges-chat-and-work/), [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-november-26-2026/), [Anthropic research note](https://www.anthropic.com/research/migrating-api-to-rust)

### 6. S&P Places Nvidia on CreditWatch Negative Amid Governance Scrutiny
S&P Global Ratings put Nvidia’s AA credit rating on CreditWatch Negative, citing management and governance risks and “key-man” concerns following recent leadership turbulence around Jensen Huang. A decision is expected within 90 days. The move comes as Nvidia continues to back large AI infrastructure financing deals.
**Sources:** [Reuters / S&P coverage](https://www.reuters.com/technology/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com), [Digest AI](https://digestai.news/today)

### 7. New York Bans AI Chatbot Toys for Children; Australia Passes Digital Duty of Care
New York Governor Kathy Hochul signed a five-year ban on the manufacture and sale of toys powered by AI chatbots. Separately, Australia’s Parliament passed a digital duty of care bill covering AI chatbots alongside social media, games, and apps, expanding the eSafety Commissioner’s powers.
