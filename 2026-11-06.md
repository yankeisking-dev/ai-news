# AI News Roundup — November 6, 2026

Clean summary of the top AI stories from the past ~24 hours (as of early November 6, 2026 IST).

## Top Stories

1. **OpenAI launches GPT-6 Cyber, rated Critical for cybersecurity**  
   OpenAI released GPT-6 Cyber, a specialized model for authorized vulnerability discovery and exploit development. It scores 48.5% on ExploitGym (vs. 32.1% for GPT-5.6 Cyber), found 81 previously unknown high-severity Firefox vulnerabilities (Mozilla patching underway), and helped develop working exploits for 948 Linux kernel bugs. Rated "Critical" under OpenAI’s Preparedness Framework and initially limited to select Daybreak Red customers via API and Codex. It shows improved scope adherence (near-zero out-of-bounds rate with prompts).  
   - [OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-cyber/)  
   - [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/openai-launches-gpt-6-cyber-with-critical-cybersecurity-rating-2026-11-05/)  
   - [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1027599/openai-gpt-6-cyber-launch)

2. **METR: Anthropic models improving roughly twice as fast, largely due to AI**  
   Independent evaluator METR published an assessment (based on high-access review Aug–Oct 2026) finding Anthropic is likely improving capabilities on well-specified tasks at about 2x the March 2024–October 2025 rate (AECI benchmark growth 1.9x since Oct 2025, 95% CI 1.5–2.8x). Roughly 80% of the acceleration is attributed to extensive internal use of AI for R&D rather than compute scaling. Much supporting evidence was redacted by Anthropic.  
   - [METR full assessment](https://metr.org/blog/2026-11-05-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment/)  
   - [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/anthropics-models-improving-nearly-twice-fast-with-help-ai-study-finds-2026-11-05/)

3. **OpenAI drops NDAs for data center projects amid public backlash**  
   Following midterm results that highlighted strong voter opposition to data center expansion, OpenAI announced new community commitments: it will not use NDAs with public officials on data center projects and will increase transparency. This comes as political resistance and local restrictions on data centers intensify, contributing to broader concerns about the AI infrastructure boom.  
   - [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1028454/openai-data-center-nda)

4. **Anthropic rolls out Claude Personal Finance beta**  
   Claude now offers a US beta Personal Finance feature for Pro, Max, and Ultra users. It lets people connect bank and card accounts via Plaid for read-only analysis of balances, spending, bills, and transactions (up to two years of history). Conversations in this hub are excluded from training; Claude cannot move or invest money.  
   - [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1027652/anthropic-claude-plaid-personal-finance)

5. **Perplexity launches Teammates, always-on work AI agents**  
   Perplexity introduced Teammates for Max subscribers (web initially). These persistent agents can take on multiple roles, pull from existing projects/skills/memory, monitor changes, collaborate, and integrate with tools like Slack. Positioned as a work-focused alternative to agents from OpenAI and Anthropic.  
   - [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1028302/perplexity-teammates-ai-agents-work)

6. **Airbnb plans AI travel agent for first half of 2027**  
   In its Q3 2026 earnings, Airbnb said it is developing an AI-native trip-planning agent expected in H1 2027. It will help users plan trips, compare options, manage bookings, and get assistance during stays, going beyond simple text chat by incorporating maps, photos, and comparisons.  
   - [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/05/airbnb-says-its-launching-an-ai-travel-agent-in-the-first-half-of-2027/)  
   - [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028412/airbnb-ai-travel-agent-q3-2026-earnings)

7. **AI-related stocks plunge as midterm results and data center concerns rattle markets**  
   Tech stocks, including the Nasdaq, saw sharp drops (Nasdaq 100 down significantly, chip index plunging) amid fears that Democratic midterm gains could slow data center construction and AI infrastructure spending. Analysts and executives noted growing public and political hostility toward the buildout that has powered recent market gains.  
   - [CNBC / related coverage]  
   - [Financial Times](https://www.ft.com/artificial-intelligence)  
   - [The Neuron daily digest](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-friday-november-6-2026/)

These developments highlight accelerating AI capabilities (especially recursive self-improvement signals and specialized cyber tools), expanding agentic and personal features, and mounting political/economic friction around the physical infrastructure required to power the boom.
