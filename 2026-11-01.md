## 1. OpenAI Cuts Funding Target to $20B After Raising ~$11B
OpenAI has secured roughly $11 billion of an original $30 billion fundraising target and pared the round to up to $20 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation (down from $1.4 trillion). ## 2. Major AI Labs Back Shared Robots.txt AI Training Opt-Out
OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, and Anthropic announced support for a shared policy token (`__usage_ai-training`) in robots.txt, proposed by Anthropic’s Kevin Kelley as an IETF Internet-Draft. Sites can signal training preferences once for multiple crawlers. Anthropic is implementing now; OpenAI in the coming months; Microsoft from Feb 1, 2027; Google aiming for 2027. It covers training only (not search/crawling) and is voluntary.

**Sources:**
- [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1023763/openai-google-microsoft-ai-scraping-standard-opt-out)
- [IETF Draft](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-kelley-aipref-training-preference-signal/)

## 3. Claude Agent Selects and Injects Gene Therapy into Live Mouse Brain
Exin Therapeutics (San Francisco) used a Claude Sonnet 5 agent to choose a gene therapy from 19 options, select brain coordinates, and control a robotic injector on a live epilepsy mouse model. A vet performed the craniotomy and held a kill switch. Claude Opus models refused the live injection step. The mouse recovered; results are not peer-reviewed. Highlights agents moving into physical lab equipment with safety limits.

**Sources:**
- [WIRED](https://www.wired.com/story/an-ai-agent-did-brain-surgery-on-live-mouse/)
- [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/a-claude-model-autonomously-chose-a-gene-therapy-and-operated-on-a-mouse-brain-with-no-human-decision-making/)

## 4. Apple Integrates Alibaba’s Qwen into Apple Intelligence for China
Apple and Alibaba announced that Qwen will power Siri, Writing Tools, and Visual Intelligence for Chinese users. Developer beta testing starts November 4, 2026; full rollout with iOS 27.3 in December. Users control activation and data sharing; requests will not train Alibaba models. Ends long regulatory delays for Apple Intelligence in China.

## 5. Google Project Suncatcher: First AI Response from Orbital TPU
Google received the first AI-generated response from a data-center-class TPU in orbit. Its Project Suncatcher prototype satellite (launched Oct 1) ran Gemma 4 and returned a haiku: “Blue marble below / Data streams in silent grace / Starlight feeds my core.” Next steps include thermal/radiation tests and twin satellites in 2027.

## 6. Chainalysis Discloses Breach via “Offensive Agentic Techniques”
Blockchain intelligence firm Chainalysis said an attacker used offensive agentic techniques to access internal systems in October, exposing customer user IDs, full names, emails, and role labels. No evidence of access to investigation content, credentials, or session tokens. The company is investigating with external experts and using LLMs for review.
