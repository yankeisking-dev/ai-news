# AI News Roundup — November 2, 2026

Clean summary of the top AI stories from the past ~24 hours (as of early November 2, 2026 IST).

## Top Stories

1. **DeepSeek open-sources proactive autonomous coding agent prototype (dsh-superagent)**  
   DeepSeek’s Harness team released an MIT-licensed research prototype on GitHub (`deepseek-ai/dsh-superagent-prototype`). It is a proactive coding agent for a single GitHub repo (with optional Feishu integration). It monitors allowed requests, opens parallel work items in dedicated sessions, and can reply with PRs. It uses a Router-Worker architecture for better autonomy under noisy conditions; internal tests showed roughly 1.9× faster p95 completion vs. single-session setups. Explicitly a research prototype, not production-ready.  
   - [GitHub repo](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/dsh-superagent-prototype)  
   - [MarkTechPost coverage](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/01/deepseek-dsh-superagent-router-worker-coding-agent/)

2. **UAE signs four major agreements with OpenAI (ADNOC first energy firm to deploy Frontier)**  
   Witnessed by Abu Dhabi Crown Prince Sheikh Khaled bin Mohamed, the deals include: ADNOC + G42/Inception42 + OpenAI making ADNOC the first energy company to deploy OpenAI Frontier for connected operational/investment/commercial decisions (on UAE inference infrastructure); a machine-readable AI-native government initiative; Mubadala for investment analysis; and a broader five-year Inception42–OpenAI agreement covering government, enterprise, and education. Builds on existing Stargate UAE infrastructure collaboration.  
   - [ZAWYA / ADNOC announcement](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/adnoc-signs-landmark-partnership-agreement-with-openai-and-g42-becoming-first-energy-company-to-deploy-openai-frontier-technology-1859105)  
   - [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/energy/2026/11/02/adnoc-signs-agreement-with-openai-and-g42-to-deploy-frontier-ai/)  
   - [WAM](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/17i6xki-khaled-bin-mohamed-bin-zayed-meets-sam-altman)

3. **South Korea proposes nearly doubling AI spending to ~$16B in record 2027 budget**  
   President Lee Jae Myung outlined an 820.9 trillion won (~$610–615B) budget (up 12.8%), allocating 21.3 trillion won (~$16B) — nearly double 2026 levels — to semiconductors, physical AI, and domestic AI data centers. Includes ~4.7 trillion won for frontier/sovereign AI models aiming for top global performance, plus infrastructure and talent programs. Leverages semiconductor tax windfall.  
   - [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/south-koreas-lee-vows-make-bold-ai-investment-record-budget-push-2026-11-02/)  
   - [Yonhap](https://en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20261102000953315)  
   - [Straits Times](https://www.straitstimes.com/asia/east-asia/south-korea-to-double-spending-on-ai-and-chips-in-2027)

4. **SemiAnalysis launches GDPx: even top models score only ~12% on hard professional agentic tasks**  
   New frontier benchmark with 206 tasks across 11 high-value domains (chip design, cybersecurity, biology, finance, legal, quant trading, etc.), built with 14 data partners. Claude Opus 5.5 leads at 12.0%, Gemini 4 Carbon 10.9%, GPT-6 Astra 9.6%. Scores remain very low; some models are constrained by safety classifiers on cyber/bio/alignment. Highlights the gap between current capabilities and real high-value professional work.  
   - [GDPx site](https://gdpx.semianalysis.com/)  
   - [SemiAnalysis newsletter](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/gdpx-measuring-frontier-model-capabilities)  
   - [Coverage summary](https://www.unite.ai/semianalysis-launches-gdpx-benchmark-spanning-11-agentic-domains/)

5. **HD Hyundai Robotics: Palantir AI agents cut robot problem resolution time 63%**  
   AI agents analyzing existing robot programs and proposing improvements reduced average fix time from 21.5 days to 7.9 days (pilot since August). Company expects >50% cut in robot programming costs. Engineers shift toward reviewing AI suggestions and acting as field solution experts. Presented at Palantir’s Sovereignty Bootcamp. Concrete physical AI industrial result.  
   - [Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10921723)  
   - [The Elec](https://www.thelec.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=15151)

6. **OpenAI to merge ChatGPT and Codex usage into one shared token pool starting December**  
   Plus, Pro, and Business plans will draw from a single token-based allowance across ChatGPT (advanced models) and Codex. Instant chat is excluded. Exact start date, pool sizes, and conversion details not yet fully published; intended as part of integrating agentic Work capabilities into main ChatGPT. Heavy coding use will compete with general chat capacity.  
   - [AI Chat Daily](https://www.aichatdaily.com/ai-tools/openai-pool-chatgpt-codex-usage-into-one-allowance)  
   - [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-chatgpt-and-codex-will-share-one-usage-allowance-in-december/)
