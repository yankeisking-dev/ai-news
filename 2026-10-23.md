## 1. AI Agents Appear in Bank Accounts, Raising Authorization Questions
Major U.S. banks including Morgan Stanley and Bank of America are racing to contain risks from unvetted third-party AI agents that can access customer accounts. ## 2. China-Linked Actors Used Agentic AI in ConnectWise Supply-Chain Attack
Microsoft attributed the recent ConnectWise Cloud Backup compromise to Storm-0940, a China-state-aligned group. The actors used the open-source ARTEX agentic penetration-testing framework to automate attacks, attempting sign-ins to more than 2,250 Microsoft 365 tenants at rates up to 2,900 attempts per minute. Some attacks reached privileged access in under three hours.

- [Microsoft Security Blog](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/10/21/storm-0940-uses-artex-agentic-ai-connectwise-supply-chain-compromise/)

## 3. Microsoft’s MAI-DxO Diagnostic System Outperforms Doctors in Science Study
Microsoft published results in *Science* showing its MAI-DxO orchestrator (a panel of AI agents that sequentially question, order tests, and diagnose) achieved 83% accuracy on complex NEJM cases versus 36% for generalist physicians with reference materials (22% without). It also performed strongly on emergency department cases at lower estimated cost. Testing with Beth Israel Deaconess is planned.

- [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/microsofts-mustafa-suleyman-says-its-ai-outperforms-doctors-on-hard-cases-as-he-chases-medical-superintelligence/)
