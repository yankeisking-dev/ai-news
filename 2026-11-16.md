# AI News Roundup – November 16, 2026

A clean summary of the top AI stories from the past 24 hours.

## 1. Jensen Huang Weighs Nvidia Comeback Amid Leadership Crisis

Days after resigning as Nvidia CEO following reports of extramarital affairs (including one with a woman alleged to have ties to Chinese intelligence), Jensen Huang is reportedly considering a return. Allies including Elon Musk have backed a petition with over 160,000 signatures calling for his reinstatement while an independent investigation continues. Nvidia CFO Colette Kress serves as interim CEO. The turmoil has contributed to volatility in AI stocks.

## 2. Anthropic IPO Draws Massive Oversubscription Ahead of Potential Record Listing

Anthropic’s IPO roadshow is reportedly 6–7 times oversubscribed. The company plans to price 825 million shares between $70 and $78 on November 18, with trading expected to begin November 19 on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. Combined with a concurrent private placement, the deal could raise over $90 billion and value the company at around $1.4 trillion, potentially the largest IPO in history.

## 3. Alibaba Officially Releases Qwen4-Max Flagship Model

Alibaba launched Qwen4-Max, a 2.4-trillion-parameter MoE model (71B activated) with native vision, a 1-million-token context window, and improved efficiency. It claims better performance than its predecessor with up to ~50% fewer tokens and ~60% lower cost per task on internal benchmarks. Available via API at reduced prices ($1.50 input / $4.50 output per million tokens). Smaller open-weight Qwen4 models are planned.

## 4. Trump Administration Plans to Name AI “National Champions”

According to The Wall Street Journal, the Trump administration intends to designate more than two dozen US AI firms—including Nvidia, Amazon, Microsoft, OpenAI, and Anthropic—as “national champions.” The goal is to help them expand overseas and compete with Chinese rivals through expedited export licenses, government support, and financing. An announcement is expected soon.

## 5. OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Wallet for Secure Agent Purchases

OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet, enabling its “dots” AI agents to complete purchases on users’ behalf. Users add a card once and approve each transaction with a biometric check (e.g., Face ID). Payment networks issue tokens so agents and merchants never see real card details. Initially available to US ChatGPT Pro subscribers. Built with American Express, Mastercard, and Visa.

## 6. OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra Assists in Physics Breakthrough; Apple Launches Health AI Study

OpenAI published work on its new science blog in which physicist Nathaniel Craig used GPT-6 Astra to help construct a candidate non-perturbative lattice definition of the Standard Model—a long-open problem. Separately, Apple launched a Health AI Usage Study with OpenEvidence to examine how people interact with AI health tools.
