# AI News Roundup – November 9, 2026

A summary of the top AI stories from the past 24 hours.

## 1. Nvidia Board Opens Independent Investigation into CEO Jensen Huang

Nvidia’s board launched an independent probe after CEO Jensen Huang admitted in a staff memo to having an affair with Alice Huang, a Megaspeed executive whom U.S. intelligence believes was a Chinese spy targeting him. Huang apologized for being unfaithful to his wife. The board stated it has no reason to believe company secrets or national security were compromised and that Huang will remain CEO while cooperating fully.

- [BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3dxw10rjn9no)
- [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/nvidia-launches-probe-after-report-ceo-huang-was-target-foreign-intelligence-2026-11-08/)
- [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/08/nx-s1-6034036/nvidia-launches-investigation-into-ceos-alleged-relationship-with-chinese-spy)

## 2. Anthropic Commits $3.4 Billion for 1.7 GW of Gas Generators

Anthropic took over orders for 1.7 gigawatts of heavy-duty Caterpillar gas generators previously placed by OnePWR Solutions, paying roughly $3.4 billion (including installation engineering). This is described as the largest known single purchase of power-generation equipment by an AI lab, aimed at securing power for data centers amid long lead times.

- [Bloomberg (citing FT)](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-08/anthropic-buys-power-for-ai-data-centres-in-3-4b-deal-ft-says)
- [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/08/anthropic-generator-deal-ai-data-centers.html)

## 3. DeepSeek Harness Agent App Hits 1.54 Million Daily Users

DeepSeek reported that its open-source agent application Harness reached 1.54 million daily active users (official API only) as of November 4, up sharply from about 271,000 in late September. Users consume roughly 92 trillion tokens per day, and the company is preparing an official plugin marketplace (with ~35,000 third-party plugins already existing).

- [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-08/deepseek-s-ai-agent-platform-hits-1-54-million-daily-users)

## 4. Tencent AI Chief Warns AI Will Soon Pose Biology Risks

Tencent’s chief AI scientist Yao Shunyu (former OpenAI researcher) warned at a Hong Kong science event that AI models’ cyber abilities—and soon their biology abilities—will reach a threshold where real harm is possible. He said biology risks may be harder to manage than cyber risks and urged open-weight labs to prioritize safety. The remarks followed Tencent’s Hy4 model launch.

- [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3373697/ai-will-soon-master-biology-unleashing-new-safety-risks-tencent-ai-chief-warns)

## 5. Amazon Forms Personal Robotics Group for New Home Robot

Amazon has assembled a Personal Robotics Group (incorporating its March 2026 acquisition of Fauna Robotics) and is hiring for a “new-to-world robot product.” The effort centers on Fauna’s Sprout, a 3.5-foot bipedal humanoid, five years after the limited Astro home robot launch.

- [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/08/amazon-home-robot-fauna-sprout.html)

## 6. Apple CEO Presses for a More Agentic Siri

Apple CEO John Ternus has pressured software chief Craig Federighi to accelerate Siri’s roadmap toward agentic capabilities that can track goals and act autonomously (competing with agents like OpenAI’s Dots, Meta’s Muse, and Grok). Incremental app-control improvements are expected sooner; fuller agent behavior could arrive next year.

- [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1029552/apple-siri-ai-agentic)

## 7. Weight-Space Programming: Editing ~1,023 Parameters Lifts Model Performance

A new paper from Shanghai AI Lab, Tongji, and Fudan University explores “Weight-Space Programming,” where a reasoning agent directly edits model parameters. On Qwen3.5 models, edits of as few as 1,023 parameters produced gains of up to 29.45 percentage points on MATH-500 (and large lifts on other benchmarks).

- [Hugging Face Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.09382)
