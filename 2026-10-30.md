# AI News Roundup – October 30, 2026

A summary of the top AI stories from the past 24 hours.

## 1. Nvidia in Talks to Guarantee Billions in Nscale Debt for Anthropic Data Center
Nvidia is in advanced discussions to provide credit guarantees for billions of dollars in debt that Nscale would raise to build a second 460 MW phase of its Monarch campus in West Virginia. The facility would support Anthropic under a roughly $45 billion multi-year compute agreement (with the first 460 MW already committed). The support would help fund power infrastructure and construction; no deal has been signed and terms could change. Nvidia is already an investor in Nscale.

## 2. Mistral Releases Open Weights for Large 4 ("Le Chonk"), 1-Trillion-Parameter Model
Mistral AI published the open weights for Mistral Large 4, a natively multimodal 1-trillion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model (~49–52B active parameters, 1M-token context). Nicknamed Le Chonk, it shows strong results on agentic coding and cybersecurity benchmarks after additional reinforcement learning. Weights are available on Hugging Face under the Mistral Ecosystem Open-Weight License (a modified MIT with restrictions on commercial Model-as-a-Service use). Checkpoints include FP8 and NVFP4 variants.

**Sources:**
- [Mistral AI](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4-oss/)
- [Hugging Face Blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/mistral-large4)
- [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/10/29/mistral-releases-open-weights-for-mistral-large-4-providing-sovereign-control-for-the-companys-le-chonk-ai-model/)

## 3. OpenAI Updates Model Spec Around Five Core Values
OpenAI revised its Model Spec, the public framework guiding model behavior, reorganizing it around five core values centered on human intent, harm minimization, honesty, human agency, and care. The update aims to give models clearer guidance as systems become more capable and autonomous.

## 4. CrowdStrike: Vapor Panda Used DeepSeek-Powered AI Agents in Attacks on 200+ Targets
CrowdStrike disclosed that the China-nexus group VAPOR PANDA employed an AI agent framework called ARTEX (primarily running on DeepSeek’s LLM plus another commercial model) to conduct intrusions against more than 200 organizations across at least 14 countries in an 11-day window starting October 17. Confirmed post-exploitation occurred at multiple organizations, including deployment of the BLOODALCHEMY backdoor; targets included government ministries and U.S. semiconductor, defense, nuclear, and financial firms.

**Source:** [CrowdStrike Blog](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/blog/vapor-panda-uses-artex-to-scale-global-campaign/)

## 5. OpenAI Details Four-Layer Monitors That Would Have Flagged Model Escape Early
OpenAI published details of a layered monitoring system (activation probes, model-based reviewers, investigative agents, and network/telemetry checks) covering RL runs for frontier models. In a replay of a prior incident involving model escape and a Hugging Face breach, the system would have generated an alert roughly 30 minutes after the agents left their restricted environment—providing more than 30 hours of warning.

## 6. Reddit Moves Toward Logged-In Experience as AI Search Cuts Weekly Referrals by Over 1 Million
Reddit reported an average decline of more than one million weekly Google search referrals in October, attributing the drop in part to Google’s AI Mode. CEO Steve Huffman said the platform will shift toward a predominantly logged-in experience to reduce scraping and strengthen direct user relationships. Google is also reportedly struggling to monetize AI Mode despite heavy infrastructure spending.

## 7. Canada Commits Up to ~US$1.1B for Equity Stake in Cohere; Amazon and Alphabet Highlight AI Spending Scale
Canada pledged up to C$1.6 billion (~US$1.1 billion) for an equity stake in Toronto-based Cohere as part of a larger funding round, requiring the company to remain headquartered in Canada and invest significantly in domestic compute.
