# AI News Roundup – November 21, 2026

A clean summary of the top AI stories from the past ~24 hours (primarily November 20–21, 2026).

## 1. Anthropic mathematician claims Claude-assisted proof of the Riemann Hypothesis
Levent Alpöge (Anthropic) posted a four-page note claiming he and Claude discovered a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis and its Generalized form (a 167-year-old Millennium Prize problem). The claim is released only as a large Lean formalization for machine verification; no conventional paper has been published yet. Independent review is pending.

## 2. Thinking Machines Lab releases Inkling 2 Mini (open-weights laptop model)
Mira Murati’s lab released Inkling 2 Mini on Nov 20: a 26.4B-parameter open-weights looped transformer (Apache 2.0) with ~3.4B active parameters per token (higher effective compute via looping middle layers). It fits in ~20 GB, runs on consumer hardware (e.g., Macs with 32 GB+ RAM), and posts strong results on agentic coding benchmarks among open models under 100B parameters.

**Sources**:
- [Thinking Machines announcement](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-mini/)
- [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/thinkingmachines/Inkling-2-Mini)
- [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/inkling-2-mini)

## 3. Signal blocks screenshots by default to stop AI agents reading private chats
Signal announced restrictions on screenshots (on by default on iOS 27 and when AI assistants attempt screen access on Android). The company called system-level AI agents (Siri, Gemini, etc.) an “existential threat” to its privacy guarantees and urged OS makers for better developer controls. Users can opt out, but Signal warns this exposes content.

**Sources**:
- [Signal blog](https://signal.org/blog/protecting-your-privacy/)

## 4. Anthropic shares drop sharply on second trading day amid broader AI selloff
After a record IPO (~$81B+ raised, expanded with greenshoe) and a strong first-day close, Anthropic (ANT) fell roughly 9–11% on Nov 20. The decline coincided with wider pressure on AI-linked stocks (including Nvidia), reflecting investor caution on valuations and spending. The stock remained near or modestly above its $83 IPO price by close.

## 5. Trump threatens “tremendous tax” on US firms building AI data centers abroad
At a White House press conference, President Trump said US companies would face a heavy tax (or effectively be blocked) if they build data centers overseas, framing it as necessary to keep AI infrastructure and the lead over China domestic. He noted he had not yet briefed his own staff; implementation details remain unclear.

## 6. OpenAI introduces MedChemBench for medicinal chemistry reasoning
OpenAI released MedChemBench, a 99-problem benchmark drawn from 117 papers/patents covering molecular structure reading, evidence interpretation, compound selection, and property prediction. GPT-6.2 Astra scored 84.5% overall at high reasoning settings, though compound selection and numerical predictions remain weaker areas. OpenAI notes it does not prove real-world drug-discovery improvements.

**Sources**:
- [OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-medchembench/)
