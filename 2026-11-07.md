# AI News Roundup — November 7, 2026

Clean summary of the top AI stories from the past ~24 hours (as of early November 7, 2026 IST).

## Top Stories

1. **OpenAI closes $11B tranche at $1.25T valuation**  
   OpenAI completed an initial $11 billion close in a funding round that values the company at $1.25 trillion, according to Bloomberg and others. This is below the roughly $1.5 trillion valuation and larger raise (up to $30B+) it had earlier sought. The remainder of the round (potentially reaching ~$20B total) is expected in the coming weeks. Investors in the first close include Coatue and T. Rowe Price, with SoftBank, MGX, Sequoia, Founders Fund, and others participating more broadly. The scale-back follows a post-midterm tech selloff and cooling investor sentiment around AI infrastructure spending.  
   - [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-06/openai-completes-11-billion-portion-of-funding-at-1-25-trillion-value)  
   - [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-secures-11-billion-funding-125-trillion-valuation-bloomberg-news-reports-2026-11-06/)  
   - [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/openai-reportedly-raises-an-additional-11-billion-at-a-1-25-trillion-valuation/)

2. **PSI system using Anthropic model proves 18-year-old Aaronson-Ambainis quantum conjecture**  
   Physical Superintelligence (PSI) announced that an internal research system running non-public Anthropic model instances produced a complete proof of the Aaronson-Ambainis conjecture (open since 2008). The conjecture concerns conditions for exponential quantum speedups. The proof was generated by day 3 of the run, verified and accepted by the Lean proof assistant, then improved twice (with Lean re-checking). A paper and Lean formalization are public on GitHub; external peer review is still pending.  
   - [PSI announcement](https://psi.inc/research/aaronson-ambainis/)  
   - [GitHub repository](https://github.com/physical-superintelligence/aaronson-ambainis)

3. **OpenAI’s GPT-6 Cyber (Critical rating) continues to draw attention for real-world vulnerability finds**  
   Released November 5, GPT-6 Cyber (a specialized variant for authorized security work) is rated Critical under OpenAI’s Preparedness Framework. It scored 48.5% on ExploitGym (vs. 32.1% for GPT-5.6 Cyber), found 81 previously unknown high-severity Firefox vulnerabilities, and helped develop working exploits for 948 Linux kernel bugs. Access remains limited to vetted Daybreak Red customers via API and Codex, with strong claims of improved scope adherence.  
   - [OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-cyber/)  
   - [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/openai-launches-gpt-6-cyber-with-critical-cybersecurity-rating-2026-11-05/)  
   - [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1027599/openai-gpt-6-cyber-launch)

4. **Meta rolls out specialized Muse models with Active Memory and introduces Spaces**  
   Meta has rolled out specialized versions of Muse and Meta AI models (trained with the Muse Wonder frontier model) to broader users, bringing Active Memory and Active Focus capabilities. The company claims large reductions in forgetting goal-critical details and better handling of changing constraints. It also launched Spaces, a new way for users to collaborate with their Muse agent by creating customized, app-like experiences.  
   - [TechCrunch (Spaces)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/meta-introduces-spaces-a-new-way-to-collaborate-with-your-muse-agent/)  
   - Coverage of model rollout across SiliconReport, The New Stack, and others.

5. **Anthropic’s Claude Personal Finance beta expands personal agent use**  
   Claude’s US beta Personal Finance tool (for Pro/Max/Ultra subscribers) lets users link bank and card accounts via Plaid for read-only analysis of balances, spending, and transactions (up to two years). Hub conversations are excluded from training; Claude cannot move or invest money.  
   - [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/05/claude-can-now-analyze-your-spending-with-its-new-personal-finance-tool/)

6. **Crusoe sells troubled Wyoming data center project to Google**  
   Crusoe sold its pre-developed AI data center site in southeast Wyoming to Google after struggling to secure anchor customers. The deal underscores ongoing challenges in the massive AI data-center buildout, including power, permitting, and local/political pushback.  
   - [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/crusoe-sells-troubled-wyoming-data-center-project-to-google/)

7. **Google DeepMind AI agents expose legacy 1024-bit RSA weaknesses**  
   DeepMind researchers (guided by cryptanalysts) reported that AI agents found two advances against legacy 1024-bit RSA in a matter of weeks: one making signature forgery roughly 1,600× more efficient on short digests, and another cutting the marginal cost of batch-factoring additional keys by ~30×. Modern RSA-2048 is unaffected; the work is framed as a demonstration of AI accelerating cryptanalysis.  
   - [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/google-deepmind-ai-rsa-1024-bit-weaknesses/)

These stories highlight continued capital flows into frontier labs (with valuation discipline), AI-assisted scientific breakthroughs, specialized cybersecurity and personal-agent features, and persistent friction around physical AI infrastructure.
