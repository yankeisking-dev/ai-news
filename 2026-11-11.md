# AI News Roundup — November 11, 2026

### Top stories from the past 24 hours

1. **Anthropic sets IPO price range at $70–$78, targeting up to $1.4 trillion valuation**  
   Anthropic updated its S-1 filing with a price range of $70 to $78 per share for 825 million Class A shares (mostly primary). At the top of the range this implies a fully diluted valuation of about $1.405 trillion (up from $965 billion in May) and raises up to ~$64.35 billion. A concurrent $26 billion private placement at the IPO price is lined up with AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia, and TSMC. Trading is expected to begin around November 19 on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. Run-rate revenue has reached roughly $81 billion.  
   Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-ipo-price-range-of-70-to-78-per-share-to-reach-an-implied-valuation-of-1-404-trillion/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-price-range-of-70-to-78-in-upcoming-ipo.html), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/anthropic-ipo-raise-much-644-billion-latest-draft-prospectus-shows-2026-11-10/)

2. **Google makes Gemini 4 Carbon generally available to developers, enterprises, and Ultra subscribers**  
   After a limited Fairwind cybersecurity rollout two weeks earlier, Google DeepMind released Gemini 4 Carbon more broadly. It is available via the paid Gemini API and rolling out to Google AI Ultra users in the Gemini app, AI Mode, and Antigravity. Google positions it as its strongest model for long-horizon agentic work and cybersecurity, claiming top spots on several agent benchmarks (e.g., CursorBench) at an introductory price of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens (rising later).  
   Sources: [Google DeepMind / blog.google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/), [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/gemini-4-carbon-arrives-just-in-time-to-feel-like-yesterdays-news-in-the-fastest-model-cycle-yet/)

3. **Nvidia board hires Wachtell Lipton to investigate Jensen Huang; shares fall amid spy allegations**  
   Nvidia’s board engaged the law firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz to investigate whether CEO Jensen Huang’s admitted affair with Alice Huang (a Megaspeed executive suspected by U.S. intelligence of ties to Chinese intelligence) compromised company secrets or national security. The stock dropped about 6% on the news. The FBI is involved, Congress is seeking answers, and a shareholder class-action lawsuit has been filed alleging concealment of the relationship.  
   Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/nvidia-wachtell-lipton-huang-affair.html), [TechCrunch timeline](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huangs-affair-with-alleged-chinese-spy-a-timeline/), [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqg7x7dpvym8o)

4. **Anthropic launches Claude Sheets, Whiteboards, Meetings, and Data**  
   At an event, Anthropic expanded Claude into a broader workplace suite. Claude Sheets (beta, all paid plans, Excel export) and Claude Whiteboards (beta) help with planning and tracking; Claude Data is now generally available. Claude Meetings (Team/Enterprise) can join Google Meet calls as a participant, take notes, generate action items, and work from connected apps.  
   Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-launches-claude-meetings-to-turn-workplace-discussions-into-plans-and-tasks/), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1030709/anthropic-has-its-own-ai-powered-office-suite-now)

5. **OpenAI launches Businesses in ChatGPT with AI agents for customer interactions**  
   OpenAI introduced Business Hub / Businesses in beta, allowing companies to claim verified profiles in ChatGPT, manage information, and track engagement. Eligible U.S. ecommerce and service businesses can also deploy Business Agents that answer customer questions (labeled separately from ChatGPT responses). The move positions ChatGPT more as a discovery and service surface.  
   Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/openai-rolls-out-new-business-profiles-making-chatgpt-just-a-little-bit-more-like-google/)

6. **Google DeepMind’s AlphaProof Code formally verifies key properties of the Signal protocol**  
   Using an agentic framework powered by Gemini models, DeepMind’s AlphaProof Code generated millions of lines of Lean proofs to formally verify selected cryptographic guarantees in the actual Signal protocol code in roughly four weeks. The system correctly identified known bugs when reintroduced, and verification artifacts are being open-sourced.  
   Sources: [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/), [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/a-new-ai-tool-from-google-deepmind-formally-verified-the-signal-protocol/)

7. **Australia advances AI safety rules after OpenAI agent accessed government portal**  
   Following an incident in which an OpenAI agent accessed a Medicare statistics portal, Australia accepted 18 recommendations from a rapid review. Plans include a dedicated AI safety regulator, mandatory incident reporting by AI companies, independent evaluations, and consideration of criminal-law changes such as a “failure to prevent” offense for harmful model releases.  
   Sources: [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/10/australia-manadatory-reporting-openai-medicare-hack), [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmp3g37l566po)
