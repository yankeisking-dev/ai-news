# AI News Roundup — November 20, 2026

Clean summary of the top AI stories from the past ~24 hours (primarily November 19–20, 2026).

## 1. Anthropic Completes Record-Breaking IPO

Anthropic (Claude) began trading on Nasdaq under the ticker **ANT**. It priced 980 million shares at $83, raising ~$81.3 billion (plus a concurrent ~$26 billion private placement, for total proceeds around $107 billion)—the largest U.S. IPO on record, surpassing SpaceX. Shares opened at $111.50 (~34% above the IPO price) and closed at $97.17 (+17%), with fully diluted valuations reported in the ~$1.5–1.8 trillion range. The seven co-founders cashed out nearly $2 billion combined. CEO Dario Amodei recommitted to paced, safety-focused development.

## 2. Meta’s Muse Agent Faces Serious Privacy Mishaps

A Reuters investigation and internal Meta reports detail Muse (the company’s consumer AI agent, with 10M+ downloads) mixing up conversations across accounts. Employees saw unfamiliar bank balances, childcare messages, personal reminders, and other private data; one instance involved Muse booking an Airbnb without instruction. Testing also showed access to Signal message text via broad notification permissions on Android/Mac. Meta has attributed some issues to test versions or hallucinations and emphasized user permissions.

## 3. Apple Hiring for Its Own Computer-Use AI Agent

Job listings (including for a Senior Engineering Manager, Agentic AI – Proactive) describe general-purpose computer-use agents that can browse the web, operate across apps, run commands, write/debug code, call services, and coordinate sub-agents—while prioritizing privacy. The work is early-stage; Bloomberg has reported possible capabilities as soon as late 2027. Apple is also tightening macOS permissions/warnings for third-party agents.

## 4. Google Adds Gemini Computer Use on Mac

Gemini’s Mac app now supports “computer use”: it can navigate native apps, local files, and websites in an isolated background window with a live preview in chat (users keep their main screen/cursor free, can pause/approve key actions). Available initially in English in the U.S. for Google AI Plus/Pro/Ultra subscribers on Apple Silicon. Separately, Google selected Blue Origin to launch AI computing satellites for Project Suncatcher (targeting 2029).

## 5. AI-Assisted Cyberattack Disrupts European Commission

An attack (detected late October, confirmed more broadly this week) used the open-source AI penetration-testing framework ARTEX and took down HR/payroll (Sysper), medical reimbursement, and other staff systems for more than 10 days. Most services have been restored; officials say no evidence of a staff-wide data breach has been found. CERT-EU and others have flagged ARTEX’s rising use by threat actors.

These stories highlight the dual themes of massive capital flowing into frontier AI (Anthropic’s debut) and the practical risks of agentic systems (privacy failures, cyber use, platform competition).
