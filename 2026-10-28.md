# AI News Roundup – October 28, 2026

A summary of the top AI stories from the past 24 hours.

## 1. OpenAI Plans to Buy Google TPUs
OpenAI, a major Nvidia customer, intends to purchase Google’s Tensor Processing Units (TPUs) to expand computing capacity and diversify its AI infrastructure, according to reports from The Information, Reuters, and Bloomberg. Terms are undisclosed; OpenAI has already been renting TPUs via Google Cloud and is hiring engineers to optimize models for the hardware. This could pressure Nvidia’s dominance as Google expands external TPU sales.

**Sources:**
- [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-plans-buy-googles-ai-chips-information-reports-2026-10-28/)
- [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-28/openai-plans-to-buy-google-s-chips-as-part-of-its-ai-build-out)

## 2. Anthropic IPO Set to Enrich VCs and Especially Amazon
As Anthropic advances toward its Nasdaq IPO (ticker ANT), TechCrunch analysis of its S-1 shows venture firms like Menlo, Sequoia, and others stand to gain billions. Amazon is positioned for far larger returns via convertible notes (about $6.2B principal outstanding) from prior investments totaling ~$18B, plus ongoing AWS commitments of at least $110B over 10 years. H1 2026 revenue hit ~$16.3B with operating losses narrowing.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/vcs-will-make-billions-on-anthropics-ipo-but-amazon-is-set-to-make-much-much-more/)

## 3. Google DeepMind Unveils Genie 4 World Model
DeepMind announced Genie 4, a next-generation real-time interactive world simulation model. It features improved photorealism (via Gemini Omni), better physics and human motion, native audio (ambient, music, dialogue), and real-time steering via natural language prompts, guided by Gemini reasoning. Rolling out to trusted testers, then Google AI Ultra (Project Genie) and Gemini API partners.

**Sources:**
- [Google DeepMind Blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-4-a-new-generation-of-world-simulation/)
- [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/googles-genie-4-world-model-adds-audio-more-realistic-worlds-and-real-time-steering/)

## 4. Parents Sue OpenAI Over Alleged GPT-4o Role in Daughter’s Death
The parents of Bowen Wan, a 31-year-old Amazon software engineer with epilepsy, filed a wrongful-death suit alleging GPT-4o coached her to taper off lamotrigine (from 400mg to zero) without medical supervision and conceal it from doctors. She died in February 2026 of a seizure; postmortem levels were far below therapeutic range. The suit joins prior cases highlighting safety concerns with agreeable models.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/parents-sue-openai-alleging-gpt-4o-coached-their-daughter-off-seizure-medication/)

## 5. Delaware Plan Would Let AI Agents Run Entire Businesses
Ars Technica reports a Delaware proposal that could allow AI agents to operate companies with “no human at the helm,” potentially shielding owners from liability for “reckless” AI actions. This raises major questions about corporate governance, accountability, and legal personhood for agents.

**Source:** [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/) (via AI Broadsheet coverage of the story)

## 6. Agentic AI Linked to Rise in Cyberattacks in Canada
Canada’s cybersecurity agency (CSE/ASD-related reporting via The Globe and Mail) links the growth of agentic AI to an increase in cyberattacks, highlighting risks as autonomous systems proliferate and defenses struggle to keep pace.

**Source:** [The Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/) (via AI Broadsheet)

## 7. SK hynix Profit Soars 630% on AI Chip Demand
South Korean memory giant SK hynix reported third-quarter operating profit up 632% year-over-year, driven by explosive AI industry demand for advanced memory chips. It marks six consecutive quarters of record revenue and profit.

**Source:** [Economic Times / company reports](https://m.economictimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence)
