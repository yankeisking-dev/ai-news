# AI News Roundup — November 14, 2026

Clean summary of the top AI stories from the past ~24 hours (primarily Nov 13–14, 2026). Focused on high-impact developments around agents, infrastructure, funding, and real-world disruption.

### 1. OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Wallet for Agent Payments
OpenAI rolled out ChatGPT Wallet, enabling its “Dots” AI agents to complete purchases on users’ behalf. Users add a card once, review the items/total/selected card, and approve each transaction with a biometric check (Face ID or fingerprint). Payment networks (Amex, Mastercard, Visa) issue tokens so neither the agent nor the merchant sees actual card details. It preserves card rewards and dispute protections. Initially limited to U.S. ChatGPT Pro subscribers (18+) with eligible U.S.-issued cards, accessed via Dots.

- Official: [OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet/)
- Coverage: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1033425/openai-chatgpt-wallet), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/openai-launches-chatgpt-wallet-letting-ai-agents-pay-purchases-2026-11-13/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/chatgpts-new-wallet-lets-ai-agents-shop-and-check-out-for-you/)

### 2. Jensen Huang Resigns as Nvidia CEO Amid Affair Allegations
Nvidia co-founder and longtime CEO Jensen Huang stepped down effective immediately after reports of extramarital affairs, including one with a Chinese businesswoman (Alice Huang) that U.S. intelligence had flagged as a possible intelligence asset, and another with a direct-report Nvidia executive. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO; the board is searching internal and external candidates. Shares dropped sharply (closing down ~4% on the day after a steeper intraday fall), with broader ripple effects on AI chip supply, export policy, and startup funding that Huang had personally championed. Trump floated Tim Cook as a possible successor; other names mentioned include Arm’s Rene Haas.

- Key coverage: [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-step-down-big-blow-ai-boom-2026-11-12/), [WIRED](https://www.wired.com/story/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-steps-down/), [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/12/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resigns)

### 3. LangChain Raises $300M Series C at $3B Valuation
The AI agent tooling company (known for its open-source framework and LangSmith platform) closed a $300 million Series C led by CapitalG (Alphabet’s growth fund), with participation from GIC, DFJ Growth, IVP, Sapphire Ventures, and others. Valuation more than doubled from ~$1.25B a year earlier. LangSmith now serves 7,000+ organizations (including NVIDIA, Salesforce, Toyota, Harvey). Funds will expand the agent management/governance platform.

- Announcement: [LangChain blog](https://www.langchain.com/blog/series-c)
- Coverage: [WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/cio-journal/ai-startup-langchain-more-than-doubles-its-valuation-to-3-billion-f83377ad), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/category/artificial-intelligence/)

### 4. FAA Nationwide Ground Stop After SMART AI Tool Expansion (AI Ruled Out as Cause)
The FAA issued a rare nationwide ground stop on Nov 13 (first full one since 2023), grounding departures for hours due to a communications outage affecting all Air Route Traffic Control Centers. It came days after expanding its $875M SMART AI traffic-management tool (from Air Space Intelligence) nationwide across all 22 centers. Officials (including Transportation Secretary Duffy) and the vendor stated the outage was caused by overloaded switches/routers in aging telecom infrastructure, not SMART, a cyberattack, or AI interference. DHS said it was still looking into the AI angle early on. Flights resumed after roughly four hours with residual delays.

- Coverage: [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/faa-grounds-all-u-s-flights-days-after-expanding-its-ai-air-traffic-system/), [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/transportation/6220416-faa-smart-ai-ground-stop-denial/), [AVWeb](https://avweb.com/aviation-news/faa-nationwide-ground-stop-lifts-smart-ai/)

### 5. Microsoft Global Outage Continues (Unrelated to Cyber or AI Agents)
Microsoft services (Outlook, Teams, and others) experienced a multi-day outage attributed to server overload. The company stated it was unrelated to cyberattacks or AI agents and reported partial recovery of consumer services while still monitoring enterprise ones.

- Coverage: [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/)

### 6. Amazon Plans Global Product Data Index to Counter AI Shopping Agents
Amazon is reportedly building a comprehensive catalog of nearly all online products (including those it doesn’t sell) to keep shoppers on its platform and blunt third-party AI agents that can shop across the web.

- Coverage: [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/)
